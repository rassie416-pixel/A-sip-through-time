A Sip Through Time

China’s Tea Story, One Cup at a Time
Same Leaf. Three Stories.

Discover China’s Tea Culture

From an ancient cultural legend to thoughtful tea etiquette and today’s creative tea drinks, explore how one leaf connects different generations and ways of life.

STORY 01 · LEGEND

Shennong and the Discovery of Tea

Chinese cultural tradition tells a story about Emperor Shennong and the discovery of tea. In the legend, tea leaves are associated with a moment of discovery that began a lasting relationship between people and tea.

This is a cultural legend, not a verified historical account.

STORY 02 · TRADITION

One Cup, One Courtesy

Traditional tea-making is about more than preparing a drink. Making and sharing tea can express respect, care and hospitality. The way tea is poured and offered helps create moments of connection between people.

STORY 03 · TODAY

From Tea Bowl to Tea Bar

Today, young people enjoy tea in many forms, including milk tea, fruit tea and visits to modern tea houses. These new ways of enjoying tea do not erase tradition; they show how tea culture continues to evolve.
